## Air Gestures 2.0

**Swipe, slide, go back.**

- ↔️ **Swipe left / right**: point the L sign to your left or right to swipe the screen (photos, stories, pages). Voice: "swipe left", "swipe right", "next page"
- 🎚 **Pinch + drag volume**: pinch, then move your hand up or down. One stroke sets the volume. A still pinch still mutes
- ◀️ **Fist = Back**, and the **open palm does nothing** by default, so there are fewer accidental triggers
- 🎨 **New app icon**: a neon hand skeleton that matches the app
- 🐛 Fixed: "scroll left" used to scroll down

### Install
Download `app-release.apk` below and open it on your Android phone (Android 8.0+). If Play Protect blocks it, see the README for the fix.
To use swipes, scrolling, double tap and Voice Autopilot, turn on the accessibility service (see README).
